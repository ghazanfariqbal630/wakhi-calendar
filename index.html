Wakhi Sholẽ Hisobet

Today

Each Wakhi month runs from the 21st of one English month to the 20th of the next, so the year opens on 21 March (Nawroz). A month has as many days as the English month it starts in. Yitoqish (Feb–Mar) has 29 days in a leap year.

Up to 2025-26 the published calendars opened the year with Yitoqish. From 2026-27 the year opens with Haba, and each year here follows the naming of its own published edition. Printed editions exist for 2020-21 to 2026-27. Years before 2020-21 use the naming of the earliest editions, and years after 2026-27 use the 2026-27 naming. Photos for those years come from the nearest edition.

On iPhone or iPad: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen to install this calendar as an app.

Add to your calendar

Show Wakhi dates inside your phone or laptop calendar (Google Calendar, iPhone, Outlook). It updates by itself when new holidays are added.

Google Calendar iPhone / Mac / Outlook
  1. Android: tap Google Calendar, sign in if asked, then tap Add. Open the Calendar app → Settings → Wakhi Calendar and turn Sync on.
  2. iPhone / Mac: tap iPhone / Mac / Outlook, then Subscribe.
  3. Laptop: Google Calendar → Other calendars + → From URL, or Outlook → Add calendar → Subscribe from web, and paste the copied link.

Phone setup

Some phones stop apps installed from Chrome to save battery, so the icon does not open. A web app is not allowed to change these phone settings itself, so do this once:

    Still not opening? Remove the app, then in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Create shortcut. The shortcut always opens.
