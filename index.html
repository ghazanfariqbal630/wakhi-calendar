Today

Each Wakhi month runs from the 21st of one English month to the 20th of the next, so the year opens on 21 March (Nawroz). A month has as many days as the English month it starts in. Yitoqish (Feb–Mar) has 29 days in a leap year.

Up to 2025-26 the published calendars opened the year with Yitoqish. From 2026-27 the year opens with Haba, and each year here follows the naming of its own published edition. Printed editions exist for 2020-21 to 2026-27. Years before 2020-21 use the naming of the earliest editions, and years after 2026-27 use the 2026-27 naming. Photos for those years come from the nearest edition.

Marked days are fixed-date occasions shown every year (Nawroz, Mazdũrẽ Ror, Takht Nẽshine Salgira, Ozodhiẽ Ror, Imomẽ Chũvake Salgira), plus occasions printed in the 2022-23 and 2023-24 editions. Eid and other moon-sighted dates are not included.
